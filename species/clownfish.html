Clownfish

Amphiprioninae

A small fish with a very specific partnership.

Status varies by species
The short version

Clownfish live in close association with sea anemones. That relationship makes them a useful way to explain how species depend on each other and on healthy reef systems.

Reef conservation is about networks, not isolated animals. Coral condition, water quality, anemones and fish communities all interact.

Main pressures
  • Damage to coral-reef habitat
  • Ocean warming and marine heatwaves
  • Unsustainable collection in some locations
What helps
  • Protect reef habitat and water quality
  • Choose responsibly sourced aquarium animals
  • Support climate and reef-restoration work
